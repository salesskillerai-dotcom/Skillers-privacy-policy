Manage or cancel your subscription

Skiller AI Pro is billed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Skiller AI to change or cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Restore a purchase

Signed in on a new phone? Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account

In the app go to Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, sessions and videos. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in Settings first.

Getting a good analysis

Film from the side, about 3–5 metres away, with your whole body and the ball in frame. Good light and a steady phone give the most accurate results.
