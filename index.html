Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Skiller AI collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information: your name, email address, and how you sign in (email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google). If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only receive Apple's private relay address.

Player profile: the details you enter during setup or in Player Profile — date of birth, gender, height, weight, playing position, strong foot, training days per week and training goals.

Videos and analysis results: the shot videos you record or import, and the results generated from them, such as body-position measurements, technique scores, estimated ball speed and coaching notes.

Usage and subscription information: how many analyses you have used, the dates you used them, your remaining free analyses, and your subscription status (plan, renewal and expiry dates).

Technical information: basic device and app information (such as device model, operating system version and app version) and your IP address, which our service providers process to run and secure the service.

How we use your information

To create and secure your account, analyze your shot videos, show your history and progress, manage your free analyses and subscription, and respond to support requests.

To prevent abuse of the free analyses, for example the same email creating new accounts to get more free analyses.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How your videos are processed

When you analyze a shot, the video is sent over an encrypted connection to our analysis server, which automatically detects your body position and the ball to produce your results. This is fully automated.

While you review the result, the video is held temporarily on our servers. If you save it, the video and its results are stored in your account. Videos you don't save are deleted from our servers automatically, normally within 30 minutes.

Service providers we share data with

We only share data with providers that help us run the app, and only for that purpose:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, database, video storage and cloud functions.
  • Our analysis server, hosted on Railway — video analysis.
  • RevenueCat — managing subscriptions and restoring purchases. It receives your account ID and purchase information, never your card details.
  • Apple — App Store payments and Sign in with Apple. Google — Sign in with Google.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment card details. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on cloud servers operated by the providers above, which may be located outside your country. Only your own account can read your sessions and videos. This is enforced by our database and storage security rules, not just by the app.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete any session and its video at any time from the History tab or the session's results screen.

When you delete your account, your profile, sessions, videos and subscription record in our database are deleted automatically.

To prevent abuse of the free analyses, we keep a one-way, irreversible hash of your email address (not the email itself), along with how many free analyses were used. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your player profile in Profile → Player Profile, delete sessions in the History tab, and permanently delete your account in Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, email sales.skillerai@gmail.com

Children

Skiller AI is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted by authentication and security rules. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and fix issues quickly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date above, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.

Questions or requests: sales.skillerai@gmail.com
